1. Introduction

Vault Heist ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect when you play Vault Heist, and how we use it.

By playing Vault Heist, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

2. Data We Collect

a) Data Stored On Your Device Only (Not Sent To Us)

  • Game progress: Current level, coins, score, high score
  • Game settings: Sound ON/OFF, Vibration ON/OFF
  • This data is stored locally on your device in user://save.dat and is NOT sent to our servers.

b) Data Collected By Third-Party Services (Google AdMob)

Vault Heist uses Google AdMob to show ads (Interstitial, Rewarded, App Open). AdMob may collect:

  • Device ID (Advertising ID)
  • IP address (approximate location for ad targeting)
  • Device model, OS version, app version
  • Ad interaction data (which ads you viewed / clicked)
  • App Open / Ad performance data

AdMob's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
Google AdMob: https://support.google.com/admob/answer/6128543

We do NOT collect your name, email, phone number, precise location, photos, contacts, or any personal information directly.

3. Children's Privacy

Vault Heist is rated Everyone and is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

AdMob is configured with:

  • Tag for Child Directed Treatment: TRUE
  • Tag for Under Age of Consent: TRUE
  • Max Ad Content Rating: G (General Audiences)

This ensures only family-friendly ads are shown.

If you are a parent and believe your child has provided personal data, contact us at info@zerometrix.net and we will delete it.

4. How We Use Data

  • To save your game progress on your device
  • To show relevant, family-friendly ads via AdMob
  • To improve gameplay (e.g., level difficulty, moves 1.5x)
  • To fix crashes and bugs

We do NOT sell your data to anyone.

5. Third-Party Services

Vault Heist uses:

  • Google AdMob (Ads) - Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services (App distribution, updates)

No other third-party SDKs collect data.

6. Data Retention

  • Local game data: Stored until you uninstall the app or clear app data.
  • AdMob data: Retained by Google per Google's Privacy Policy.

7. Your Choices

  • You can opt out of personalized ads: Android Settings → Privacy → Ads → Delete Advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization
  • You can turn off Sound / Vibration in game Settings
  • You can remove ads: In-app purchase "Remove Ads ₹199" in Settings (if available)
  • You can uninstall the app at any time to delete local game data

8. Security

Your game data is stored only on your device. We use no servers to store your data, so there is no server breach risk.

We may update this Privacy Policy. The new version will be posted at this URL with a new Last Updated date.
